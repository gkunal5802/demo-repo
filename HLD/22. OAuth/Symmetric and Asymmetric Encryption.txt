Symmetric and Asymmetric Encryption:

Symmetric key:
1. Same key is used for encryption and decryption
2. Algo used: DES and AES.
DES: is fixed length(56 bits) and easy to crack. Process data in 64 bits and perform 16 rounds in its encryption process.
AES: length (can be 128, 192, 256 bits). process data in 128 bits and perform 10,12,14 rounds in its encryption process

Advantages: Ideal for bulk data because faster than asymmetric encryption due to smaller length
Disadvantages: Secure key distribution is difficult.

Asymmetric Encryption:
1. Different keys (public and private) are used for encryption and decryption
2. Algo used:
- RSA: supports 2048 bits length
- DSA
- Diffie-Hellman
- ECHDE

Pros: 
- Two keys are used, so no issue of security distribution of key.
- Diffie-hellman, ECHDE provides key exchange protocol.
- Digital signature can be created easily.

Cons: Computation intensive and slower encryption than symmetric key.
