High Availability & Resilience System

question can be asked in following ways:
1. Design High Availability or Resilience System
2. Design a system that can avoid 99.999% failure or single point failure
3. Design Active-Passive/Active-Active design architecture

High Availability architecture is used to avoid single point of failure of application. We have 2 approaches to achieve this-

1. Active-Passive
-> In this, we have two or more datacenters with their separate DBs, but only one of them is treated as Primary until it fails or gets down.
If primary fails, then our datacenters will make connection with one of the other replica and it will be treated as Primary.

Note: We cannot write into replica DBs because Oracle/SQL/PostgreSQL doesn't support multi-master approach. But we can read from replica DBs.

Disadvantages:
1. Latency: It occurs when application from passive Data centers tries to read and write to active Datacenters.
2. Fault Tolerance delay: The system goes into 5-15mins of failure while switching context from primary to replicas during Failure of Primary.

2. Active-Active
In this, all the datacenters are treated as active, read and write can happen on any datacenter. It usually uses NoSQL because that support multi-master architecture. Synchronization happens all the time among all the DBs.

Note: Sometimes, Conflict occurs, when writing to two DBs at the same time and trying to syncup or one DB writes and other one is reading at same row.
